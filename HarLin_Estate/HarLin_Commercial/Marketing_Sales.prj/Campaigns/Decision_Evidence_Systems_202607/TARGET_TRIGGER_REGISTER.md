## Wave 2 — observed-trigger mining tests

Target an event, not a company list. Candidate triggers:

1. acquisition, farm-in or divestment announced;
2. capital raise linked to a new technical programme;
3. maiden or materially revised resource programme;
4. unexpected drilling result or strategy reset;
5. board/management change requiring portfolio review;
6. entry into PNG, Pacific or another frontier jurisdiction;
7. critical-minerals funding, strategic partnership or supply-chain work;
8. consultant appointment or vacancy signalling temporary capability pressure.

For each event, create a why-now brief with: public source, date, decision likely faced, plausible sponsor, relevant HarLin proof, consented route and a single useful question. Do not manufacture a problem from a share-price movement alone.
