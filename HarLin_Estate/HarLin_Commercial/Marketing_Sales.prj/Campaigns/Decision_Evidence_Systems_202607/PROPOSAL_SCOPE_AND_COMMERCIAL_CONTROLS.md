# Proposal, Scope and Commercial Controls

## One-page proposal structure

1. **Decision and why now** — the client's stated decision, deadline and consequence.
2. **Resolved state** — what the decision owner will possess at completion.
3. **Included inputs** — source classes, volume band, interviews and jurisdictions.
4. **Deliverables** — named artefacts and delivery session.
5. **Method** — ingest, evidence map, analysis, adversarial challenge, review and readout.
6. **Exclusions and reliance** — regulated opinions, specialist work, implementation and unlisted evidence.
7. **Schedule and client dependencies** — clock starts only after inputs, owner and access are complete.
8. **Fixed fee and payment** — approved by Paul.
9. **Acceptance and change control** — objective completion event and one correction round.
10. **Authority to proceed** — signature/acceptance and commencement payment.

## Scope builder

| Dimension | Base scope | Expansion requiring price/time review |
|---|---|---|
| Decision | One named decision or deliverable | Additional decisions or materially different audiences |
| Evidence | Agreed source set and volume band | New data room, late sources or unstructured media outside band |
| Interviews | Up to two nominated sessions | Additional stakeholders or repeated elicitation |
| Jurisdiction | One primary operating/reporting context | Additional legal/regulatory regimes |
| Output | One principal product plus evidence/issues ledger | Multiple decks, reports, models or submission-ready versions |
| Review | One consolidated factual-correction round | New analysis, changed question or conflicting reviewer rounds |
| Timing | Standard agreed window after complete inputs | Rush, weekend or reserved-capacity delivery |
| Specialist input | Client supplies/approves or separately quoted | Legal, cyber, valuation, tax, audit, translation or local expert work |

## Internal rate and effort guardrails

Fixed pricing is preferred. These controls test whether the fixed fee is commercially viable; they are not public rate commitments.

- Known opportunity-cost reference: current Barton base AUD 1,250/day.
- Target Paul contribution after the first two pilots: generally at least AUD 350 per Paul-hour before tax and corporate overhead for bounded premium work.
- Do not quote below the published pilot floor without recording the strategic consideration received.
- Include at least 20% internal effort contingency until two paid deliveries calibrate rework.
- Maximum three simultaneous delivery scopes during validation; maximum one urgent/rush scope.

## Price decision worksheet

`Approved fixed fee = base offer + evidence complexity + interview/review load + urgency + specialist/hosting costs + risk contingency - explicit pilot consideration`

Record:

- forecast Paul-hours;
- forecast production/automation hours and external costs;
- implied value per Paul-hour;
- commencement payment;
- cash dates;
- downside if inputs are late or poor;
- what is removed if the client has a lower budget.

## Default commercial position for testing

- Australian prices exclude GST where applicable.
- New client: 50% commencement and 50% on delivery; 100% upfront below AUD 5,000 may be used.
- International client: meaningful cleared commencement payment before access/work; quote currency and bank/FX treatment explicitly.
- Seven-day proposal validity where capacity or urgency is material.
- Client warrants authority to provide inputs and identifies confidential, personal, privileged or export-controlled material.
- No external publication or third-party reliance unless explicitly included.
- HarLin may use no client name, data, artefact, testimonial or case fact without separate written permission.

## Acceptance criteria by offer

### Decision Compression

Accepted when the agreed memo, evidence/assumption ledger, unresolved-question set and readout are delivered against the named decision. Agreement with the recommendation is not an acceptance criterion.

### Data-Room Triage

Accepted when the agreed evidence index, contradiction/red-flag register, missing-information questions and prioritised further-work plan are delivered for the bounded source set.

### White-label Decision Product

Accepted when the partner's nominated approver confirms the agreed product reflects the supplied evidence and one consolidated factual-correction round is incorporated.

### AI Control Map

Accepted when the bounded system/use-case inventory, owner/data/permission map, selected control/evidence crosswalk and prioritised gap plan are delivered. It does not certify compliance.

### Digital Estate Diagnostic

Accepted when the bounded inventory, value/risk classification, canonical/version findings and rescue roadmap are delivered. No migration or deletion is implied.

## No-go contract conditions

Escalate or decline if the client requires:

- an unsupported regulated, audit, legal, tax, valuation, resource or cyber-certification opinion;
- unlimited evidence, revisions, stakeholder access or continuing support for a fixed fee;
- responsibility for decisions while withholding material inputs;
- unsafe handling of sensitive information or unapproved third-party tools;
- public use of HarLin's conclusion outside the agreed reliance context;
- payment contingent solely on a transaction, investment outcome or regulatory acceptance without legal review;
- a warranty that AI-assisted work is error-free.
