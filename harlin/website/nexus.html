Data truth architecture

The program observes. The geologist adjudicates.

Four rules, enforced in code, that make NEXUS trustworthy enough to sign.

Source-of-truth cascade

Every value resolves through a priority chain — hard logged data, then as-drilled, then planned. Downstream cards read through the cascade automatically. You never re-type what the platform already knows.

Advisory, never coercive

Validation flags what disagrees; it never blocks, never overwrites. The driller's numbers stay the driller's numbers. Inconsistencies queue for review at your pace, with a full audit trail.

Provenance on everything

Every auto-populated value shows where it came from; every AI suggestion records model, confidence and your accept/reject. The audit pack writes itself.

Declared, never assumed

End-of-hole is what you declare — the program reconciles it against the deepest logged data and raises a flag on mismatch. Software that guesses is software you can't sign against.
