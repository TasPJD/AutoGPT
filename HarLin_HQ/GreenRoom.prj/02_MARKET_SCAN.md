## 1. The landscape (as known, early 2026)

| Player | What it is | Video avatar call? | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yoodli** | AI speech/interview coach; analyzes your recording or live roleplay; strong analytics | Partial (roleplay is mostly voice/text-driven UI, not a convincing "person") | Feels like a *tool*, not a *counterpart*. Low fear-response. |
| **Google Interview Warmup** | Free text/voice Q&A practice | No | Free but shallow; no pressure, no follow-ups, no coaching depth |
| **Big Interview** | B2B2C course + recorded mock practice (universities/career centers) | No (canned video prompts, you record answers) | One-way; no live interaction; dated UX |
| **Final Round AI / Interview Copilot class** | Real-time answer feeding DURING real interviews + some mock practice | Some mock modes | Ethically radioactive; we position as the integrity alternative |
| **Huru, Interviewsby.ai, etc.** | Mobile mock-interview Q&A apps | No | Question-drill, not conversation |
| **Speeko / Orai / Poised** | Speech-delivery coaching (pace, fillers) | No | Delivery-only; no counterpart, no content coaching |
| **Toastmasters / human coaches** | The incumbent for speaking practice | n/a | Scheduling, cost ($100-300/hr), judgment anxiety — our core wedge |
| **Avatar API vendors themselves** (Tavus, HeyGen, Anam) | Sell "AI interviewer" as a demo vertical to enterprises (HR screening) | Yes | They sell screening-of-candidates B2B; almost nobody owns *candidate-side practice* with a photoreal live counterpart |

**The gap:** live, photorealistic, pressure-inducing *counterpart* + serious coaching loop,
sold to the candidate/speaker, not the employer. Most incumbents analyze; few simulate.
The simulation-quality window opened recently — incumbents built their UX before
sub-second photoreal avatars existed, and their architectures show it.

## 2. Positioning

**"The flight simulator for high-stakes conversations."**
Pilots don't read about landings; they fly simulators. You don't read interview-question
lists; you sit the interview. Realism is the product; coaching is the retention; integrity
(practice, never cheat) is the brand.

Wedge order:
1. **Job interviews** (MVP) — sharpest trigger, Paul is the test user, clear persona
   library (HR screen, technical panel, hostile board, salary negotiation).
2. **Public speaking / pitch** (fast follow) — audience-panel mode, Q&A-after-talk mode.
3. Later verticals if pulled: media training, PhD vivas, board presentations, visa/
   immigration interviews, medical OSCE roleplay (each is persona + rubric, same engine).

## 3. Pricing landscape (hypothesis, refresh before G2)
- Human mock interview with a coach: $80-300/session — our price umbrella.
- Yoodli-class SaaS: ~$10-30/mo consumer, B2B seats higher.
- Sensible opening posture: free first session (the hook is the spectacle; COGS of one
  free session is a marketing cost) → credit packs (~$5-15/session) → pro subscription
  for heavy users/speakers. Credits before subscription: matches episodic demand.

## 4. Why we can win with $0 marketing (standing decision compatible)
- The share artifact (before/after card) targets LinkedIn, where the audience *is* the
  market and career-content organic reach is strong.
- "I got grilled by an AI interviewer" demo clips are inherently spectacular — the product
  demos itself in 15 seconds of screen recording.
- Paul's own senior-hiring network is the seed beta cohort (recruiters, career coaches,
  outplacement firms are also the eventual B2B channel: coaches assigning homework).
